Two honest options. Neither is marked correct.

Every item is something a thoughtful person might do either way. You get a check-in on what you noticed, something you already did, and something you might do. Nothing is scored. If a situation didn’t come up, you say so, and it counts as nothing at all.

Noticed
A check-in on what you noticed today.
Did
Something you already did, answered honestly.
Might
Something you might do, if this came up. Two options, and a way to skip.

Five concepts, each a real tension.

Not virtues to score. Each day you meet one item from each, and your answers place you somewhere between two poles a thoughtful person could reach for.

The gap is the insight.

Patterns marks two points on each concept: what you would do, and what you did. Most people predict they will be more patient, or more honest, than they turn out to be. Patterns shows that distance instead of hiding it.
