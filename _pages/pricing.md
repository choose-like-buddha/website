# Pricing

**{{ site.app_name }} is free to download and free to use.** There are no ads on
either plan, and you don't need an account to start. The optional
{{ site.premium_plan_name }} raises the daily limits and unlocks the three deeper
breathing techniques — you never need it to build the habit.

{% include pricing-table.html %}

## Do I need the {{ site.premium_plan_name }}?

Probably not at first. Five questions a day is already more than most people
finish, and the free Sama breathing is the technique you'll reach for most. The
upgrade is for when the daily limit starts getting in your way — usually after
the habit has taken hold.

## How billing works

The subscription is monthly at {{ site.premium_price }}, billed by the App Store
or Google Play rather than by us. Cancel it from your device's subscription
settings at any time, and it stays active until the end of the period you've paid
for. There is no annual plan and no one-time purchase.

Prices are shown in USD. Your store displays the price in your local currency
along with any applicable tax.
